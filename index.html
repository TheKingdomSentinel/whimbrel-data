Whimbrel ResearchData

The US medtech market, machine-readable

Five years of FDA clearances and approvals, NIH awards, patents, and SEC filings. Refreshed every morning, every event linked to its public source, and the people each registry names on the record.

Files

  • index.json: machine-readable manifest of everything here
  • signals-latest.json: buying signals from the last 14 days
  • llms.txt: this site, for machine readers
  • stats.json: aggregate history and archive scale (aggregates only)
  • methodology.md: how the data is collected, dated, and windowed, what it holds about people, and what these files exclude
  • signals-archive.json: the complete archive as one file, priced by the x402 catalogue

The files above are a rolling 14-day window, except the archive file, which is everything. The archive is also queryable free, a search or a company at a time, below.

Query it by MCP

The same data answers over the Model Context Protocol (Streamable HTTP). The free tools need no account and no key:

https://whimbrel-signals-mcp.whimbrelresearch.workers.dev

Free tools: latest_signals, signal_of_the_day, weekly_pulse, who_got_funded, find_signals, micro_brief, company_timeline, about_the_data, get_access. find_signals searches the whole archive; micro_brief and company_timeline are capped at 100 company lookups per caller per day. Company records also carry patent grants, published patent applications, and SEC funding filings. The server ships seven ready-made whimbrel_-prefixed prompts, so clients that surface prompts as slash commands get a command palette on connect.

Setup steps for Claude, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other MCP clients: https://whimbrelresearch.com/connect/. Any client that speaks Streamable HTTP can use the URL above directly.

Query it by HTTP

The same handlers answer GET at the same origin, for readers that do not speak MCP:

/v1/signals/latestthe last 14 days of signals, filterable by kind
/v1/signals/searchsearch the whole archive
/v1/signals/fundedthe window filtered for fresh money
/v1/signals/todaythe newest single signal
/v1/pulseseven-day aggregates
/v1/company/{company}every registry event for one company
/v1/company/{company}/briefa composed mini-dossier, as markdown

/v1/subscribe redirects to Stripe checkout for the paid tier, and /v1/key/{session} hands over the resulting API key once.

Discovery and terms are free and stay free: the x402 catalogue lists every route, its price, and whether payment is currently switched on, and /v1/about states the terms in prose. Read the price from the catalogue rather than from this page.

The paid layer

A Whimbrel key unlocks the rest of the same server: deep_record, research_status, matches_for_us, build_profile, set_preferences, billing_portal. deep_record runs the research pipeline on any company you name, research_status tracks the run, and build_profile, set_preferences and matches_for_us match the archive against your firm's own profile. get_access is free and needs no key: it returns a checkout link, and the page after paying shows an API key once. Send the key as an Authorization: Bearer header. billing_portal handles card changes, upgrades and cancellation. What the paid layer is and who it serves: https://whimbrelresearch.com/connect/.
