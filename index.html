Whimbrel Research: medtech signals data

Daily-updated, machine-readable US medtech buying signals. Every fact links to its public source.

  • signals-latest.json: NIH awards and FDA clearances/approvals from the last 7 days
  • hot.json: companies with a fresh buying-window event
  • llms.txt: this site, for machine readers
  • methodology.md: how the data is collected and verified

Free to cite with attribution. The full corpus and company-to-firm matching are the service: whimbrelresearch.com · nate@whimbrelresearch.com
