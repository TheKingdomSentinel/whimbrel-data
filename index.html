Whimbrel ResearchData

The US medtech market, machine-readable

Five years of FDA clearances and approvals, NIH awards, patents, and SEC filings. Refreshed every morning, every event linked to its public source, and the people each registry names on the record.

Files

  • index.json: machine-readable manifest of everything here
  • signals-latest.json: buying signals from the last 14 days
  • llms.txt: this site, for machine readers
  • stats.json: aggregate history and archive scale (aggregates only)
  • methodology.md: how the data is collected, dated, and windowed, what it holds about people, and what these files exclude

These files are a rolling 14-day window. The archive behind them runs back years and is queryable a search or a company at a time, below.

Query it by MCP

The same data answers over the Model Context Protocol (Streamable HTTP). The free tools need no account and no key:

https://whimbrel-signals-mcp.whimbrelresearch.workers.dev

Free tools: latest_signals, signal_of_the_day, weekly_pulse, who_got_funded, find_signals, micro_brief, company_timeline, about_the_data, get_access. find_signals searches the whole archive; micro_brief and company_timeline are capped at 25 company lookups per caller per day. Company records also carry patent grants, published patent applications, and SEC funding filings. The server ships seven ready-made whimbrel_-prefixed prompts, so clients that surface prompts as slash commands get a command palette on connect.

Claude (web and apps): Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector → paste the URL above.

Claude Code:

claude mcp add --transport http whimbrel https://whimbrel-signals-mcp.whimbrelresearch.workers.dev

Cursor: Settings → MCP → Add new server, or add to ~/.cursor/mcp.json:

{"mcpServers": {"whimbrel-signals": {"url":
"https://whimbrel-signals-mcp.whimbrelresearch.workers.dev"}}}

VS Code (Copilot): add to .vscode/mcp.json:

{"servers": {"whimbrel-signals": {"type": "http", "url":
"https://whimbrel-signals-mcp.whimbrelresearch.workers.dev"}}}

Other MCP clients: any client that speaks Streamable HTTP can use the URL directly.

Query it by HTTP

The same handlers answer GET at the same origin, for readers that do not speak MCP:

/v1/signals/latestthe last 14 days of signals, filterable by kind
/v1/signals/searchsearch the whole archive
/v1/signals/fundedthe window filtered for fresh money
/v1/signals/todaythe newest single signal
/v1/pulseseven-day aggregates
/v1/company/{company}every registry event for one company
/v1/company/{company}/briefa composed mini-dossier, as markdown

/v1/subscribe redirects to Stripe checkout for the paid tier, and /v1/key/{session} hands over the resulting API key once.

Discovery and terms are free and stay free: the x402 catalogue lists every route, its price, and whether payment is currently switched on, and /v1/about states the terms in prose. Read the price from the catalogue rather than from this page.

The paid layer

A Whimbrel key unlocks the rest of the same server: deep_record, research_status, matches_for_us, build_profile, set_preferences, billing_portal.

Research on demand. deep_record points the research pipeline at any company you name and runs it on our infrastructure: current leadership, contact routes, funding and milestones read from the company's own site and press, every line quoted from the source it came from.

Matching, from your own profile. build_profile reads your firm's website and drafts your capability profile, set_preferences stores your screening decisions, and matches_for_us returns the companies in the archive that fit what your firm actually does. Onboarding is self-serve through those three.

Buying is self-serve too. get_access is free and needs no key: it returns a Stripe checkout link, and the page you land on after paying shows an API key once. Send that key as an Authorization: Bearer header. billing_portal returns the Stripe portal for card changes, upgrades and cancellation.
